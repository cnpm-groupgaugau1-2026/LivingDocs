# 4\. Responsibility Assignments

To ensure clear accountability, task ownership, and seamless collaboration, responsibilities across the project lifecycle are assigned to the 5 team members using the **DRSI Matrix model**:

- **D (Driver / Direct):** Primary owner directly leading and executing the task.
- **R (Reviewer / Responsible):** Secondary owner participating, reviewing, or co-executing the task.
- **S (Support):** Supporting role providing technical inputs, data, or assistance.
- **I (Informed):** Role kept updated on progress and outcomes.

1. **Tien (Leader / FE):** Leader / Frontend Lead & Product Owner.
2. **Quynh (Backend / DB):** Backend & Database Lead.
